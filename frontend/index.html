What you can do

Everything students and the placement cell need during campus recruitment.

Profile and resume

Add your skills, education and projects. Upload a PDF resume and we suggest what to put on your profile — you choose what to keep.

Job recommendations

See the jobs open to your course ranked by how many of the required skills you already have, and which ones you are missing.

Company listings

Browse companies with their job type, package, requirements and eligible courses. Search and filter to find the right fit.

Application tracking

Apply with one click and follow each application from pending to accepted or rejected, along with your overall placement status.

Resume review

The placement cell reviews every resume before you can apply, so companies only see resumes that are ready.

Events and notifications

Placement drives, workshops and mock interviews in one calendar, with a notification whenever something changes.

How it works

From registration to an offer, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Register

    Create an account and choose your course. Your course decides which companies you see.

  2. 2

    Complete your profile

    Add your skills and upload your resume. Review the suggestions we find in it and keep what is right.

  3. 3

    Get approved and apply

    Once the placement cell approves your resume, apply to the companies that suit you.

  4. 4

    Track your progress

    Your status moves from Under Review to Interview Scheduled to Placed, and you are notified at each step.
